# Media Production Planning Workflow

## Goal

Deliver a completed promotional video for a fictional campaign while coordinating script development, filming, editing, graphics, audio, review, and final delivery.

---

# Mechanisms

The project will be managed through:

- A shared task tracker.
- Weekly production status updates.
- Clear task ownership.
- Milestone reviews.
- Risk and dependency tracking.
- Final quality approval.

---

# Phases

## Phase 1 — Pre-Production

### Tasks

- Define the project objective.
- Confirm target audience.
- Develop and approve the script.
- Prepare the production schedule.
- Confirm required resources.
- Confirm filming location.

---

## Phase 2 — Production

### Tasks

- Prepare camera and lighting equipment.
- Confirm production team availability.
- Conduct filming.
- Review captured footage.
- Organize and store production files.

---

## Phase 3 — Post-Production

### Tasks

- Begin video editing.
- Record and provide voice-over.
- Produce motion graphics.
- Synchronize audio and video.
- Create the first complete draft.

---

## Phase 4 — Review and Revision

### Tasks

- Review the first draft.
- Collect stakeholder feedback.
- Identify required revisions.
- Assign revision tasks.
- Track completion of revisions.

---

## Phase 5 — Final Delivery

### Tasks

- Complete the final quality review.
- Confirm approval.
- Export final media files.
- Organize project files.
- Close completed tasks.
- Document lessons learned.

---

# Project Milestones

| Milestone | Completion Requirement |
|---|---|
| Script Approval | Final script approved |
| Production Ready | Location, team, and equipment confirmed |
| Filming Complete | Required footage captured and reviewed |
| First Draft Complete | Editing, graphics, and audio combined |
| Final Approval | Required reviewers approve the final version |
| Project Delivered | Final media files delivered and documented |
