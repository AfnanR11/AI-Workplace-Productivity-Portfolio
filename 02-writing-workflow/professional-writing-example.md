# Professional Writing Workflow

## Scenario

A media production task is delayed, and I need to send a professional update to the production team.

---

## 1. AI-Generated Draft

**Subject: Project Delay**

Hello Team,

The project is delayed because some tasks are not finished. Please finish your work as soon as possible because we need to complete the project.

Thanks.

---

## 2. Fact Verification

Before using the draft, I reviewed:

- Which tasks were actually delayed.
- Whether the delay affected the entire project or only one production activity.
- The responsible team members.
- The current production schedule.
- Whether a new completion date had been confirmed.

No specific dates were added unless they were confirmed by the project manager.

---

## 3. Tone Refinement

The original draft was too direct and did not provide enough context.

I refined the message to:

- Maintain a professional and respectful tone.
- Clearly explain the purpose of the message.
- Ask for an updated status.
- Avoid assigning blame.
- Focus on solving the production issue.

---

## 4. Human Review and Sign-Off

I reviewed the final message to ensure that:

- The project information was accurate.
- The tone was appropriate for the team.
- No confidential information was included.
- The requested action was clear.

**Status: Human Approved**

---

## 5. Final Version

**Subject: Follow-Up on Production Progress**

Hello Team,

I would like to follow up on the current progress of the outstanding production tasks.

Please provide an update on the status of your assigned work and let me know if there are any blockers or dependencies affecting completion.

If the expected completion timeline has changed, please share the updated estimate so that I can review the impact on the overall production schedule.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,  
Media Production Project Manager
